Uses rating, role balance, individual form, recent team-result form and proven teammate fit to minimize the overall team mismatch.
Team Builder 2.0: individual PWT Rating v1.1 is only the starting point. The engine also compares fragging, support, aggression, recent form, experience/confidence and teammate chemistry.
